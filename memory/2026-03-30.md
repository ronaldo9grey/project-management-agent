## 技术要点

### 类型转换问题
- `projects.leader_id` 是 `integer` 类型
- `personnel.employee_id` 是 `character varying` 类型（如 '0001'）
- 查询时需要转换：`int(employee_id.lstrip('0') or '0')`

### project_tasks.project_id 类型
- `project_tasks.project_id` 是 `character varying` 类型
- 查询时需要：`WHERE CAST(project_id AS INTEGER) = :project_id`
